## Motion

- **Tools opens from its button.** Match the button's position to the panel's transform origin. Use one quick expansion, then a short stagger as the tool groups arrive. Closing returns to the same place. Shorten or skip travel for reduced motion.
- **Tool groups move through a consistent depth transition.** Opening a group moves the next choices forward. Back reverses the direction. Search results update in place to keep typing immediate.
- **Layers stay spatial.** Preserve the live 3D card paths. In the next pass extend the same motion to the transition from the full picture into the deck. Selected cards stay readable while parked. Pinch remains bounded and the recording surface stays separate.
- **Sliders attach to the thumb after the hold.** Keep the current hold-to-edit behavior. Refine the arming highlight and let the value follow the thumb as soon as the slider is armed. Scroll, tap and hold remain distinctly different interactions.
- **Record stays unmistakable.** Give readiness, countdown, locked recording and take saved their own concise motion and status. Keep the continuous capture surface and native save flow. Honor reduced motion and preserve keyboard controls throughout.

## Delivery

1. **Design branch slice.** Connect the compact dock and Tools picker to actual instruments, including Camera FX, recording Library and advanced controls. Verify the live camera and rendering engine stay behind the picker. Preserve the native menu and specialized workspaces.
2. **Editor pass.** Add a shared inspector pattern for Camera FX, native visual controls and the layer editor. Move primary controls up, let related controls expand in place and keep every current value, label and reset reachable. Add search synonyms based on the existing guide and measure time to find common controls.
3. **Performance pass.** Carry the motion into Layers, portrait and mirrored landscape, capture and save. Refine touch cancellation and interruption. Add a compact search view for small phones and an orientation-aware approach to camera and panel movement.
4. **Release check.** Walk a fresh user through opening a scene, finding and editing a Camera FX, recording in both orientations and saving a take. Walk a returning user through import, pinned controls, BYOS, layer reordering and advanced export. Compare screenshots and timings to main. Check keyboard navigation, touch targets, reduced motion, browser Back, active recording and real iPhone and Android performance before promoting to production.

Keep animation work local to the controls and their surfaces. Favor transform and opacity and interruptible transitions. Budget extra motion around the live GPU work. Verify smoothness with Camera FX and recording active because an empty demo can hide performance problems.
