@mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.32 — Apache License 2.0
Source: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Local vision worker and WASM runtime. Segmentation/face/pose model files are
fetched on first use from Google's MediaPipe model distribution and cached
on the user's device. Camera frames are processed locally.
